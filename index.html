The Truth About Buying Tayrona Park Passes

Don't fall for online scams. Understand how official entrance tickets actually work.

🛑

Warning: Official Park Passes Cannot Be Reserved Online in Advance

Do NOT trust third-party websites claiming to sell official park entry tickets on the internet.

By regulation of Parques Nacionales Naturales de Colombia, official park entrance passes are paid strictly in person at the park gates (El Zaino or Palangana entrances) in cash or credit card upon arrival.

⚡

How to Avoid Buying Tickets In Person & Skip the Queue:

When you reserve a guided tour with us, you avoid all of this hassle! Your certified local guide arrives at the gate early in the morning, handles the ticket booth queue, and buys your entry passes and mandatory insurance for you. When you arrive, your guide takes you directly into the park entrance without waiting in line.

🎫 Entry Fees (2026 Rates)

  • Foreign Tourists: ~90,000 COP ($22 USD)
  • Colombian Citizens / Residents: ~35,000 COP ($9 USD)
  • Mandatory Daily Medical Insurance: ~6,000 COP/day ($1.50 USD)

📄 What You Must Bring to the Gate

  • Original Physical Passport or Colombian Cedula
  • Cash (COP) or credit card for entry & insurance fees
  • No single-use plastics allowed (Strict Eco Policy)

Top Destinations in Tayrona National Park

Plan your route from the main El Zaino entrance to iconic beaches.

🏝️ Cabo San Juan del Guía

2.5 hr hike from entrance

The iconic twin-cove beach featuring the famous hammock hut on the granite hill. Safe for swimming, calm turquoise waters.

🌊 Arrecifes Beach

1.5 hr hike from entrance

Dramatic coastline with massive granite boulders. Strong dangerous currents—swimming is prohibited, but scenery is breathtaking.

🌴 La Piscina

2 hr hike from entrance

A natural reef barrier creates a tranquil, pool-like bay perfect for swimming and snorkeling with colorful tropical fish.

🐒 Pueblito Chairama Trail

3.5 hr jungle trek

An ancient Tayrona indigenous stone village nestled in the high jungle. High chance of spotting howler monkeys and toucans.
